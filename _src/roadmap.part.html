The longer view

Foresight should not be a luxury good

Large hospital groups have had demand planning for decades. They have it because they can afford the analysts, not because their problem is harder. A twelve-item clinic in a small town faces the same question — what will we need, and when — with none of the support.

That gap is the whole reason Regnum exists. If a piece of software that runs on a five-year-old desktop can close most of it, then the only remaining barrier is whether somebody bothers to build it well.

We intend to.
